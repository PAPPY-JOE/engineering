I am a **Mechatronics Engineer and Robotics Researcher** interested in building autonomous robotic systems that can perceive, reason, and act reliably in real-world environments.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **robotics, computer vision, autonomous navigation, embedded AI, and control**. I am particularly interested in how robots can combine visual perception with learning and control to operate safely and effectively under uncertainty and computational constraints.

I graduated from **Bowen University** with a **B.Eng. in Mechatronics Engineering, First Class Honours (CGPA: 4.89/5.00)**, ranking **1st in a cohort of 50 students**. During my undergraduate studies, I developed a strong interest in research-driven robotics and autonomous systems, with projects spanning computer vision, embedded machine learning, and robotic navigation.

My undergraduate thesis focused on **GRACE, an autonomous healthcare service robot**, where I developed a vision-based navigation and semantic scene-understanding system using **ORB-SLAM3, OCR, OpenCV, and a Raspberry Pi 5**. The project explored how resource-constrained robots can build spatial understanding and recognize meaningful locations in healthcare environments without relying on external infrastructure.

I have also worked on **embedded computer vision and TinyML**, including an edge-based plant classification system using the **Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3 Sense and Edge Impulse**. As team lead, I coordinated a six-person team that collected and processed a campus-scale image dataset, trained and evaluated lightweight image-classification models, and deployed the resulting model for on-device inference. I later presented this work at the **TinyML4D Academic Network Show and Tell**.

My research journey has also taken me to international academic communities. I was selected as an **AAAI-24 Undergraduate Consortium Scholar**, one of 19 students selected worldwide, and received travel support to attend the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Vancouver. I have also received travel support and awards associated with **CVPR, ECCV, and ACL**, experiences that strengthened my commitment to pursuing research in robotics and artificial intelligence.

> **I am interested in robots that are not only intelligent, but also capable of operating reliably, safely, and meaningfully in the physical world.**
